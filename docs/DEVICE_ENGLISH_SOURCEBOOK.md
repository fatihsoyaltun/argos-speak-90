# Device English Sourcebook: ForenScope 8K

## Purpose

This sourcebook prepares controlled, device-specific English for possible future Argos Speak 90 curriculum integration. It covers only the ForenScope 8K product described in the existing sourcebook and the presentation extraction.

This phase does not change the app curriculum. It does not add SuperSpectral content. It does not add unsupported device features.

### Evidence-Level Labels

| Label | Meaning in this sourcebook | Curriculum rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **verified technical fact** | A concrete name, component, format, count, or safety statement is explicitly listed in the source material. This label does not mean independent laboratory validation. | May be used with its source context and without expanding the claim. |
| **source-stated capability** | The manufacturer presentation describes the device or software as having this capability. | Use cautious wording such as “the source states,” “is designed to,” or “can support.” |
| **marketing claim** | Promotional or performance language appears in the source without supporting test evidence. | Do not use as a plain factual training statement. Attribute and label it if discussed at all. |
| **unclear / needs verification** | The source lacks enough detail, test data, named documentation, or a defined interface to verify the claim. | Keep out of factual curriculum until a suitable source is obtained. |

## Product and Source Boundary

### Source-Supported 8K Names

Use these names:

- **8K**
- **8K Latent Fingerprint Detection Tablet**
- **8K mobile compact system**
- **8K System**

The phrase **“8K ANDROID Latent Fingerprint Tablet”** is the presentation filename/title wording. It is not the preferred product name in this sourcebook. Do not overuse **“8K Android Latent Fingerprint Detection Tablet”** as though it were the only official product designation.

### Contactless LITE Boundary

Contactless LITE appears with the 8K in the product family overview (presentation slide 8; earlier sourcebook p.9). It is a **separate product**. Slides 9–10 of the presentation concern Contactless LITE and are outside the 8K extraction scope.

Do not transfer Contactless LITE specifications, features, examples, or claims into 8K device English. A product-family reference does not prove that the two products share the same capabilities.

### AFIS Control Rule

Presentation slide 21 describes this sequence under “How to send it to AFIS?”:

1. Use monocolor camera/invert mode.
2. Maintain a 1:1 ratio.
3. Capture RAW photography.
4. Perform minutia calculation.
5. Continue to identification.

This is an **AFIS submission/preparation workflow**. The presentation does not name an AFIS partner, API, certified interface, or automated connection. It does not prove direct AFIS integration.

Use:

- AFIS-related workflow
- AFIS preparation
- manual AFIS submission workflow
- prepare an image for AFIS submission

Do not use:

- connects directly to AFIS
- automatically sends to AFIS
- certified AFIS integration
- guaranteed AFIS match

## 5. UVC Safety Language

Presentation slide 19 states that the 8K contains UVC light, that UVC can be harmful to the human body, and that it should be used with the darkroom supplied in the box. This is the only formal safety statement in the presentation extraction.

### Safety Vocabulary

| English term | Simple Turkish meaning | Controlled sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UVC safety warning | UVC güvenlik uyarısı | Please follow the UVC safety warning. |
| harmful | zararlı | According to the slide, UVC can be harmful to the human body. |
| supplied darkroom | ürünle birlikte verilen karanlık oda | Use UVC with the supplied darkroom. |
| UVC exposure | UVC maruziyeti | Follow approved safety instructions for UVC exposure. |
| operator safety | operatör güvenliği | Confirm the UVC safety step before operation. |

### Required Operator Instructions

- Before selecting UVC, confirm that the supplied darkroom is in place.
- Use the UVC setting with the supplied darkroom.
- Do not present UVC as safe for unrestricted use.
- State clearly: “According to the presentation, UVC can be harmful to the human body.”
- Follow the supplied documentation and the organization’s approved safety procedure when more detailed instructions become available.

This source is not a complete UVC safety/compliance document. It does not provide exposure limits, enclosure specifications, personal protective equipment requirements, interlock behavior, or emergency procedures.

### Customer Q&A

**What does the 8K do?**

The source describes it as a contactless system for latent fingerprint detection and imaging.

**Does it need powder or chemicals?**

The manufacturer presentation describes a workflow that does not use powder or chemicals to detect latent evidence. This is a source-stated capability, not an independent performance result.

**Does it touch or damage evidence?**

The source describes the workflow as contactless. The current materials do not independently validate every evidence-handling outcome, so do not promise that evidence can never be affected.

**What imaging ranges are listed?**

The presentation lists UVA, UVB, UVC, visible, and infrared imaging capability.

**Is UVC safe to use anywhere?**

No. According to the slide, UVC can be harmful to the human body and should be used with the supplied darkroom.

**Can the 8K connect directly to AFIS?**

Direct integration is not documented in the available material. The presentation describes a manual AFIS preparation/submission workflow using monocolor/invert mode, a 1:1 ratio, RAW photography, and a minutia-calculation step.

**Can it scan from three meters?**

The presentation states remote scanning “up to 3 meters on the surface without any processing.” No test method or field-performance data is shown, so this is a manufacturer-stated, unverified range rather than a guaranteed result.

**Is it accredited for US crime-gun cases?**

The presentation makes an accreditation statement, but it does not name an accrediting body, agency, certificate, or scope. The claim needs verification before customer-facing or curriculum use.

**Does it always work on the demonstrated surfaces?**

The presentation shows examples on several surfaces, but it provides no success rate, sample size, test methodology, or chain-of-custody data. The examples should not be treated as independent validation or a guarantee.

**Is Contactless LITE another name for the 8K?**

No. Contactless LITE appears in the product family overview, but it is a separate product. Its claims must not be mixed with 8K claims.

## 7. Future Curriculum Controls

### Safe for Later Curriculum Drafting

The following material is sufficiently controlled for future drafting, provided its evidence level and cautious wording are preserved:

- Source-supported 8K product names.
- Latent fingerprint, evidence surface, contactless, and chemical/powder-free vocabulary.
- The source-listed 45.9MP camera description, without inferring exact pixel dimensions.
- UVA, UVB, UVC, visible, and IR vocabulary.
- Names of the ten light groups and seven motorized filters.
- Android control software vocabulary.
- RAW/TIFF capture, RAW video, listed export formats, stamps, geo-tag, reporting, archive, enhancement, and live-zoom vocabulary.
- AFIS preparation and manual AFIS submission workflow language.
- Presentation-demonstrated surface vocabulary, clearly labeled as demonstration examples.
- The mandatory statement that UVC can be harmful and should be used with the supplied darkroom.

### Must Not Enter Curriculum Yet

- “World’s first and only” as a factual statement.
- Any guarantee of “no image distortion.”
- “Third level identification” as a proven identification result.
- The three-meter remote-scanning range as a guaranteed field outcome.
- The US crime-gun accreditation claim as fact.
- “6 continents / near 100 countries” as verified company reach.
- Direct, automatic, or certified AFIS integration.
- Guaranteed AFIS matching or identification.
- Accuracy rates, success rates, or “always detects” language.
- Exact light/filter settings for a surface.
- Detailed UVC exposure, compliance, or emergency procedures not present in the source.
- Contactless LITE specifications presented as 8K specifications.
- Any SuperSpectral content.

### UVC Requirement for Every Future Learning Pack

Any future lesson, dialogue, demo, troubleshooting exercise, Q&A, or operator instruction that includes UVC must also state:

1. UVC can be harmful to the human body according to the slide.
2. UVC should be used with the supplied darkroom.

## Final Device-English Verdict

This sourcebook is ready as a **controlled, presentation-backed 8K language source** for limited future curriculum drafting. It is suitable for product naming, hardware/software vocabulary, cautious feature explanations, UVC safety language, demonstrated-surface vocabulary, reporting phrases, and manual AFIS preparation language.

It is not a verified operator manual, safety/compliance guide, performance validation report, accreditation record, or AFIS integration specification. Marketing claims, unverified performance statements, direct AFIS integration, exact settings per surface, and certification claims must remain outside factual curriculum until stronger sources are obtained.

No app curriculum content is changed by this sourcebook.
