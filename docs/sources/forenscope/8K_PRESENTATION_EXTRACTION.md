## 3. Hardware and Imaging System

- **Camera:** 45.9MP, described as "8K" resolution class (Slide 11). No sensor brand/model, lens spec, or resolution units (pixels vs. true 8K video standard) given — **needs verification** whether "8K" refers to sensor output naming or actual 8K (7680×4320) equivalence.
- **Light sources:** 10 light groups: White, UVC, UVC-Pw (power/high-output UVC), UVA, UVA-Pw, Blue2, Cyan, Green, Red, IR2 — arranged in a "Ring Light System Standard" (Slide 17).
- **UV/VIS/IR capabilities:** Full-spectrum claim: UVA-UVB-UVC, visible, and IR imaging (Slide 11). UVC specifically marketed for suppressing background patterns/writing and revealing prints on varied and aged surfaces (Slide 13).
- **Filters:** Motorized filter wheel, 7 filters named on Slide 18 (see table above) — enables narrow-bandpass and long-pass imaging for different wavelength ranges.
- **Physical design:** "Tablet" form factor (per product name); "mobile compact system" (Slide 11); "carry the lab in your hand" (Slide 7, marketing phrasing).
- **Mobility:** Lithium-ion battery powered, usable in field (CSI) and lab (Slide 7).
- **Contactless workflow:** Explicitly stated as 100% contactless, non-contaminative, non-destructive (Slide 7). Remote scanning up to 3m claimed (Slide 15) — **distance claim unverified, no test methodology shown.**
- **Evidence handling implications:** Because the device is marketed as contactless/non-destructive/chemical-free, source implies it avoids physically altering evidence — this is a source-stated capability, not independently verified in this deck.
- **Safety statements:** Slide 19 explicit warning: "8K contains UVC light, which can be harmful to the human body, please use it with the darkroom that comes with in the box." This is the **only formal safety statement** found in the deck — treat as verified/required operator guidance.

**Technical operator instruction:**
- "Select the appropriate light group and filter combination for the surface type before capturing."
- "Always operate the UVC setting inside the supplied darkroom enclosure — UVC exposure can be harmful without it." (safety-critical, source-stated)
- "For AFIS submission, switch to monocolor/invert mode, maintain 1:1 ratio, and capture in RAW before running minutia calculation."

**Troubleshooting / clarification:**
- "If the image looks washed out under UVC, confirm the correct narrow-bandpass filter is engaged."
- "If background text or patterns are still visible, the UVA setting alone may be insufficient — try UVC." (source-stated: "UVA light is not enough to detect fingerprints")

**Professional reporting language:**
- "The latent print was imaged using [light/filter combination], captured in RAW format, and stamped with date, location, and geo-tag metadata per the device's automated stamping feature."
- "Image was exported in [format] for archival and further AFIS-preparation workflow."

**Customer Q&A:**
- Q: "Does this damage the evidence?" A: "The system is designed as contactless and does not require powder or chemicals, per the manufacturer." (hedge: manufacturer-stated, not independently tested by us)
- Q: "Can this connect directly to our AFIS?" A: "The device supports a manual workflow (monocolor/invert capture, 1:1 ratio, RAW) to prepare images for AFIS submission — direct integration is not documented in this material and should be confirmed with ForenScope."

## 7. Risk and Claim-Control Section

**Safe to use in customer-facing English (directly source-stated, low risk):**
- Camera spec: 45.9MP, UV/VIS/IR imaging capability
- Contactless, chemical/powder-free detection method
- 10 light groups + motorized 7-filter system
- Android-based control software with listed export formats and stamping features
- UVC safety requirement (darkroom enclosure)

**Requires cautious wording ("may help," "can support," "source states"):**
- "World's first and only system" — say "ForenScope states this is..." not "This is..."
- "No image distortion" — say "the source materials describe imaging with minimal distortion" rather than asserting as fact
- "Third level identification" quality — say "the source claims sufficient detail for advanced identification levels; independent verification recommended"
- Remote scanning "up to 3 meters" — present as a stated spec range, not a guaranteed field result
- "Accredited for crime gun cases in the US" — present only as "ForenScope states its technology has been used/accredited in US crime-gun cases," and flag for verification since no accrediting body is named

**Should NOT be used until independently verified:**
- Any implied AFIS *direct integration/interoperability* certification — the deck only shows a manual prep workflow, not a certified interface
- Success-rate, accuracy-rate, or "always detects" type claims — none are quantified in the source, so none should be invented
- Any specific accrediting agency/certificate name for the crime-gun claim (not provided in source)

**Vague marketing phrases to keep OUT of Argos Speak 90 directly:**
- "Carry the lab in your hand"
- "World's first and only system in its field"
- "Crystal clear fingerprint image" (Slide 13 — descriptive, not measurable)
- "100 countries," "6 continents" (unverified scale claims, not device-technical)

## 9. Final Extraction Verdict

- **Is this presentation sufficient for a verified device sourcebook?** Partially. It reliably documents hardware components (camera spec, light groups, filters), software feature list, and demonstrated surface types. It does **not** provide performance data, test methodology, accuracy/success rates, named certifications, or detailed operating procedures (e.g., exact settings per surface, step-by-step capture sequence, error states).
- **What is missing:**
  - A user/operator manual with step-by-step procedures and settings per surface type
  - Technical datasheet with exact sensor model, resolution in true pixel dimensions, battery runtime, weight/dimensions
  - Any named accreditation/certification documentation for the "crime gun cases" claim
  - AFIS integration documentation (is it a certified interface, or manual image prep only?)
  - Safety/compliance documentation beyond the single UVC warning slide
- **What additional document would improve accuracy:** ForenScope 8K user manual / technical datasheet / safety data sheet (SDS) for the UVC module, and any accreditation certificates referenced on Slide 23.
- **Is this source safe for curriculum integration?** Yes, **for hardware/software feature vocabulary and demonstrated-use-case language**, provided every claim retains its evidence-level label (source-stated vs. marketing) as tagged above. It is **not** sufficient on its own for statements about accuracy, certification, or AFIS interoperability — those need the missing documents before being stated as fact in training content.
