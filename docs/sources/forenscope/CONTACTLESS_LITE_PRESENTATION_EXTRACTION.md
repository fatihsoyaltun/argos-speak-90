- "Environment friendly" (no powder/chemicals) — manufacturer claim, general device-line statement

- "World's first multispectral reflective fingerprint detection system on reflective surfaces" — superlative, no supporting evidence in deck

- "Multispectral" as a technical descriptor — no wavelength/band specification given; do not imply a specific spectral range

**Marketing phrases to keep out of curriculum (as literal text):**
- "World's first…"
- "carry the lab in your hand"
- "Non-stop real mobility"
- Any unqualified "100%" framing without the manufacturer-attribution caveat
