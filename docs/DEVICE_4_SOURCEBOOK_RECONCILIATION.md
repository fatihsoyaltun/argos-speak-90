# Device-4: 8K Sourcebook Reconciliation

## Scope

Reconciled `docs/DEVICE_ENGLISH_SOURCEBOOK.md` against `docs/sources/forenscope/8K_PRESENTATION_EXTRACTION.md`. No app curriculum, UI, Supabase/Auth/Admin/cloud-sync logic, existing extraction, or SuperSpectral content was changed.

## What Was Corrected

- Replaced the overused long product name with source-supported names: **8K**, **8K Latent Fingerprint Detection Tablet**, **8K mobile compact system**, and **8K System**.
- Defined Contactless LITE as a separate product that appears only in the product family overview; its claims must not be mixed with 8K claims.
- Added the evidence labels **verified technical fact**, **source-stated capability**, **marketing claim**, and **unclear / needs verification**, with curriculum rules for each.
- Reframed AFIS content as an **AFIS-related workflow**, **AFIS preparation**, or **manual AFIS submission workflow**. The available source does not prove direct AFIS integration.
- Strengthened the UVC warning across safety vocabulary, operator instructions, troubleshooting, customer Q&A, and future curriculum controls: UVC can be harmful to the human body according to the slide and should be used with the supplied darkroom.
- Kept the “up to 3 meters on the surface without any processing” statement only as a source-stated, unverified range—not a guaranteed field result.
- Labeled application surfaces as presentation demonstrations rather than independent laboratory validation. The presentation provides no success rate, sample size, test methodology, or chain-of-custody data.
- Added cautious A2, B1, technical operator, troubleshooting, professional reporting, and customer Q&A language.
- Added the missing-source list: operator manual, technical datasheet, UVC safety/compliance document, AFIS integration documentation, accreditation documentation naming the agency and scope, and exact settings per surface.

- Guaranteed distortion, identification, detection, accuracy, success, or three-meter field performance
- Crime-gun accreditation as fact
- Direct, automatic, or certified AFIS integration
- Exact settings per surface
- Detailed UVC safety procedures not present in the source
- Contactless LITE claims presented as 8K claims
- SuperSpectral content

## Readiness Verdict

It is not sufficient as an operator manual, performance validation, UVC compliance guide, accreditation record, or AFIS integration specification. Stronger source documents are required before those claims or procedures can enter factual curriculum.
